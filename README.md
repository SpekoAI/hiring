# Engineering at Speko

Speko is OpenRouter for Voice AI. Speko helps developers build voice apps that talk and can make and receive phone calls. We benchmark speech models in public and use those measurements to choose your model. The work is measurement, routing and real-time infrastructure, in Go, TypeScript and Python, and every published number must be one a stranger can re-run.

Before you apply, look at the real work: the open-source gateway repository ([github.com/SpekoAI/gateway](https://github.com/SpekoAI/gateway)), [benchmarks.speko.ai](https://benchmarks.speko.ai) (it publishes a data.json and an llms.txt), and [docs.speko.dev](https://docs.speko.dev).

Apply through [the form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScr36iwQRena6sUX9HKrT7SdvPJl8QDVHO_0TPeTrZwKd9Abg/viewform); it takes five to ten minutes. The rest of this page explains every step.

## Who this is for

Senior engineers who have owned and run a production system and can defend a number in English. The seats are full time. We are too early for interns or juniors. If that is you today, come back when you have run something in production for real users; send the link then, and we will read it.

## How we hire

We start with a small build, not an interview.

1. Send one link to the best thing you shipped, plus your CV, through the form. About five to ten minutes.
2. If the link is real and yours, and the region line below fits you, you get three short written questions and a request for a one-minute voice note in English. About an hour to ninety minutes.
3. If those pass, you get a build task. It takes one or two days of your time inside the week after you get it. You build from a clean checkout on a normal laptop, with no paid key from us, and you end with a three to five minute recorded walkthrough in your own voice. The task text tells you what is required and what counts as a fail before you start, and it arrives with the date of your written review. If the review is a pass, one written question about your numbers follows; a paragraph is enough.
4. Only after we have run your build do we talk: one video call. Nobody gets a call before that, including people we know. No puzzles, no whiteboard, and a pass is a call, not an offer.

## Where and when

The team builds from Tashkent and works Tashkent hours (UTC+5). We are remote today; an office is planned, not open. This round we hire in Central Asia. Based elsewhere? Tell us where you are and whether Tashkent hours work from there.

## What we promise, and what we do not

- Every step tells you its cost before you take it, and every step after the form tells you its date. The whole process runs over several weeks. If you hold another offer with a deadline, tell us in your written answers and we tell you in writing whether our dates fit.
- Every complete application (one link plus a CV) gets a written answer. Every build task is read by a person, and the review carries at least one specific technical observation, whatever the outcome.
- If a posted date slips on our side, the new date and the reason go on this page the same week. Every posted date carries a fallback date beside it, and you hear from us by that fallback date.
- Asking a scope question in writing before you build counts in your favor, never against you.
- Use any AI tools you like, and tell us which. Be ready to explain every decision in your own words; part of the call runs without an AI coding assistant. We do not run AI-text detectors on anything you write.
- We ask for no work beyond the steps listed above. We do not run contests, and we do not promise paid trials or start dates from this page or from a talk.
- Accent is never scored. The recording and the call test whether you can defend a number in English.
- Your build stays yours. Nothing you make for a task is used in production.
- At the end we ask you one question about this process, and we read the answer.
